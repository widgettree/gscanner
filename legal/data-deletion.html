GScanner Data Deletion

Updated 19 October 2026

Privacy policy | Terms of use | Data deletion

No account to delete

GScanner does not create user accounts or a developer-operated cloud document library. These instructions explain removal of locally stored information and copies you choose to export. Google SDK diagnostic data is handled separately under Google’s policies.

Delete selected documents

Move a document to Trash, then open Settings → Trash and choose Delete forever. Moving to Trash alone does not permanently remove a document. Page images referenced by another document, duplicate or extracted selection remain until those references are removed. Some older edit files can remain in private storage until you clear all app storage.

Delete exports and saved presets

Use Settings → Clear export copies to remove prepared export copies in the app. Delete saved signatures and watermark presets using their delete controls in the corresponding editor tools. Files saved outside the app, backup ZIPs, cloud copies, recipient copies and print jobs are separate and must be deleted using the relevant file manager or service.

Remove all local GScanner data

First export anything you want to keep. In Android Settings, open Apps → GScanner → Storage, then Clear storage or Clear data; wording varies by device. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. This removes the local library, Trash, recognized text, presets, preferences, temporary files and recovery copies held in app storage. It does not delete files already saved to external storage, cloud providers or other apps. This action cannot be reversed without a separate backup.

Diagnostics and support enquiries

The developer cannot delete Google-controlled ML Kit or Play services telemetry using local document controls. Consult https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google privacy options and retention practices. If you previously sent a support enquiry, contact the developer through the listing contact details to ask about that correspondence. The developer cannot remotely inspect or recover a private library that has not been sent to them.

Contact

For privacy questions, data enquiries or support, use the developer contact details on the GScanner Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.widgettree.gscanner. Do not send scans, identity documents or signatures unless they are necessary to explain your enquiry.
